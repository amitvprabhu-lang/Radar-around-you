China has 6,220 objects in Earth orbit in CelesTrak's whole catalogue of 13 November 2026, 07:20 UTC: 1,611 active satellites, 89 inactive satellites, 288 rocket bodies, 4,226 pieces of debris and 6 unknown objects.
