The Moon is a waxing gibbous and 98 percent lit at Sun 25 Oct 00:00. Tonight it is up at nightfall, then sets in the west-northwest at Sun 25 Oct 05:49. It is highest at 23:48, 47° up in the south. New, first quarter, full and last quarter each cover about 22.5 degrees of the cycle; crescent and gibbous cover about 67.5 degrees each.
