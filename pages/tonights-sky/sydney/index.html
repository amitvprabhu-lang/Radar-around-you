The Moon is new and 1 percent lit at Mon 12 Oct 00:00. Tonight it is up at nightfall, then sets in the west-southwest at 19:45. It is highest at 19:05, 7° up in the west-southwest. New, first quarter, full and last quarter each cover about 22.5 degrees of the cycle; crescent and gibbous cover about 67.5 degrees each.
