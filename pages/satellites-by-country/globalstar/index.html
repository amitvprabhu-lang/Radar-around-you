How many satellites and how much debris does Globalstar have in orbit?

Numbers last changed 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC (orbit data of 11 Oct, 02:30 UTC, catalogue of 10 Oct, 08:25 UTC). CelesTrak's catalogue records 85 objects in Earth orbit for Globalstar: 27 active satellites, 57 inactive satellites, 0 rocket bodies and 1 piece of debris.

CelesTrak updates the catalogue about once a day and the orbit data about every 2 hours; the times are in the line above.

What this means

The catalogue records these objects under the owner "Globalstar" (code GLOB). That ranks 14th of 106 owners, after South Korea (88) and ahead of Canada (81). Its 1 piece of debris is under 0.1 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit.

The 2-hourly orbit data agrees: 27 active. Across the catalogue, 93.4 percent of the 12,525 pieces of debris in Earth orbit carry the owner of a satellite from the same launch; 826 do not; the owner does not say who or what broke a piece off (what the owner of debris means).
