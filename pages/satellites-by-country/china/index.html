Satellites, rocket bodies and debris in orbit

China has 6,188 objects in Earth orbit in CelesTrak's whole catalogue of 31 October 2026, 08:30 UTC: 1,576 active satellites, 73 inactive satellites, 284 rocket bodies, 4,229 pieces of debris and 26 unknown objects. That debris is 33.7 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit, the most of any owner. The 2-hourly orbit data of 31 October 2026, 21:10 UTC has 1,571 active (5 fewer; why they differ).
