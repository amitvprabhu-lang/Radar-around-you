Satellites, rocket bodies and debris in orbit

China has 6,171 objects in Earth orbit in CelesTrak's whole catalogue of 23 October 2026, 10:41 UTC: 1,561 active satellites, 73 inactive satellites, 281 rocket bodies, 4,230 pieces of debris and 26 unknown objects. That debris is 33.7 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit, the most of any owner. The 2-hourly orbit data of 24 October 2026, 14:10 UTC has 1,557 active (4 fewer; why they differ).
