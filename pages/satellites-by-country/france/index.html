How many satellites and how much debris does France have in orbit?

Numbers last changed 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC (orbit data of 11 Oct, 02:30 UTC, catalogue of 10 Oct, 08:25 UTC). CelesTrak's catalogue records 640 objects in Earth orbit for France: 76 active satellites, 53 inactive satellites, 160 rocket bodies, 350 pieces of debris and 1 unknown object.

CelesTrak updates the catalogue about once a day and the orbit data about every 2 hours; the times are in the line above.

What this means

The catalogue records these objects under the owner "France" (code FR). That ranks 5th of 106 owners, after United Kingdom (725) and ahead of Japan (318). Its 350 pieces of debris are 2.8 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit; 83 of them are catalogued under one launch, 1989-006. 6 more objects that have not re-entered are recorded around other bodies (orbit centre SU 6) and are not counted here.

The 2-hourly orbit data of 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC has 70 active (6 fewer; why they differ). Across the catalogue, 93.4 percent of the 12,525 pieces of debris in Earth orbit carry the owner of a satellite from the same launch; 826 do not; the owner does not say who or what broke a piece off (what the owner of debris means).
