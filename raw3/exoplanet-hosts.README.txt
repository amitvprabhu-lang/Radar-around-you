raw3/exoplanet-hosts.csv: NASA Exoplanet Archive, table pscomppars (Planetary Systems Composite Parameters), the planets whose host
star has a Hipparcos name. Fetched 2026-10-05 with the TAP service:

https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/TAP/sync?query=select+pl_name,hostname,hip_name,sy_pnum,sy_dist,disc_year,discoverymethod+from+pscomppars+where+hip_name+is+not+null+order+by+hip_name,pl_name&format=csv

Acknowledgement the Archive asks for (https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/acknowledge.html): "This research has made use of the NASA
Exoplanet Archive, which is operated by the California Institute of Technology, under contract with the National Aeronautics and Space
Administration under the Exoplanet Exploration Program." The Archive's page did not state a data licence in the text read.
The archive's column list says hip_name is the star's name in the Hipparcos Catalog and sy_pnum the number of confirmed planets.
