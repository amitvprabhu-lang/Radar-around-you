How many satellites does the United States have?

As of 17 October 2026, 22:30 UTC, the catalogue records 12,567 active satellites for the United States, 75.0 percent of the catalogue's active satellites. The whole catalogue lists 18,412 objects in Earth orbit for the United States, 3,908 of them debris.

What makes up this fleet

Recorded as "United States", the largest fleet among 99 owners. Starlink makes up 11,151 of them; the other 1,416 are not Starlink.

The United States has 18,412 objects in Earth orbit in CelesTrak's whole catalogue of 17 October 2026, 08:56 UTC: 13,023 active satellites, 802 inactive satellites, 678 rocket bodies, 3,908 pieces of debris and 1 unknown object. That debris is 31.2 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit, the 2nd most of any owner. The 2-hourly orbit data of 17 October 2026, 22:30 UTC has 12,567 active (456 fewer; why they differ).
