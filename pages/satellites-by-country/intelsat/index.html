How many satellites and how much debris does INTELSAT have in orbit?

Numbers last changed 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC (orbit data of 11 Oct, 02:30 UTC, catalogue of 10 Oct, 08:25 UTC). CelesTrak's catalogue records 149 objects in Earth orbit for INTELSAT: 36 active satellites, 64 inactive satellites, 0 rocket bodies and 49 pieces of debris.

CelesTrak updates the catalogue about once a day and the orbit data about every 2 hours; the times are in the line above.

What this means

The catalogue records these objects under the owner "International Telecommunications Satellite Organization (INTELSAT)" (code ITSO). That ranks 9th of 106 owners, after India (195) and ahead of ESA (118). Its 49 pieces of debris are 0.4 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit; 36 of them are catalogued under one launch, 2016-053.

The 2-hourly orbit data agrees: 36 active. Across the catalogue, 93.4 percent of the 12,525 pieces of debris in Earth orbit carry the owner of a satellite from the same launch; 826 do not; the owner does not say who or what broke a piece off (what the owner of debris means).
