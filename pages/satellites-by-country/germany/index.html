How many satellites and how much debris does Germany have in orbit?

Numbers last changed 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC (orbit data of 11 Oct, 02:30 UTC, catalogue of 10 Oct, 08:25 UTC). CelesTrak's catalogue records 100 objects in Earth orbit for Germany: 75 active satellites, 25 inactive satellites, 0 rocket bodies and 0 pieces of debris.

CelesTrak updates the catalogue about once a day and the orbit data about every 2 hours; the times are in the line above.

What this means

The catalogue records these objects under the owner "Germany" (code GER). That ranks 11th of 106 owners, after ESA (118) and ahead of Italy (95). No debris in Earth orbit is recorded under this owner. 3 more objects that have not re-entered are recorded around other bodies (orbit centre SU 3) and are not counted here.

The 2-hourly orbit data of 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC has 69 active (6 fewer; why they differ).
