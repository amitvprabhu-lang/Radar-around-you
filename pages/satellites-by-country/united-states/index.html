How many satellites does the United States have?

As of 20 November 2026, 16:42 UTC, the catalogue records 12,668 active satellites for the United States, 74.9 percent of the catalogue's active satellites. The whole catalogue lists 18,529 objects in Earth orbit for the United States, 3,913 of them debris.

What makes up this fleet

Recorded as "United States", the largest fleet among 99 owners. Starlink makes up 11,206 of them; the other 1,462 are not Starlink.

Satellites, rocket bodies and debris in orbit

The United States has 18,529 objects in Earth orbit in CelesTrak's whole catalogue of 19 November 2026, 16:55 UTC: 13,135 active satellites, 799 inactive satellites, 681 rocket bodies, 3,913 pieces of debris and 1 unknown object. That debris is 31.2 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit, the 2nd most of any owner. The 2-hourly orbit data of 20 November 2026, 16:42 UTC has 12,668 active (467 fewer; why they differ).
