The Moon is a waning crescent and 2 percent lit at Sun 8 Nov 00:00. Tonight it rises in the south-southeast at Sun 8 Nov 08:31. It is highest at Sun 8 Nov 08:44, 0° up in the south-southeast. New, first quarter, full and last quarter each cover about 22.5 degrees of the cycle; crescent and gibbous cover about 67.5 degrees each. So "first quarter" here means 40 to 60 percent lit, and another almanac may name the night differently.
