How many satellites and how much debris does EUTELSAT have in orbit?

Numbers last changed 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC (orbit data of 11 Oct, 02:30 UTC, catalogue of 10 Oct, 08:25 UTC). CelesTrak's catalogue records 62 objects in Earth orbit for EUTELSAT: 28 active satellites, 34 inactive satellites, 0 rocket bodies and 0 pieces of debris.

CelesTrak updates the catalogue about once a day and the orbit data about every 2 hours; the times are in the line above.

What this means

The catalogue records these objects under the owner "European Telecommunications Satellite Organization (EUTELSAT)" (code EUTE). That ranks 19th of 106 owners, after Spain (64) and ahead of Türkiye (44). No debris in Earth orbit is recorded under this owner.

The 2-hourly orbit data agrees: 28 active.
