Satellites, rocket bodies and debris in orbit

China has 6,189 objects in Earth orbit in CelesTrak's whole catalogue of 6 November 2026, 20:31 UTC: 1,580 active satellites, 90 inactive satellites, 285 rocket bodies, 4,227 pieces of debris and 7 unknown objects. That debris is 33.7 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit, the most of any owner. The 2-hourly orbit data of 7 November 2026, 16:30 UTC has 1,577 active (3 fewer; why they differ).
