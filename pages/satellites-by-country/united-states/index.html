How many satellites does the United States have?

As of 31 October 2026, 21:10 UTC, the catalogue records 12,628 active satellites for the United States, 75.0 percent of the catalogue's active satellites. The whole catalogue lists 18,487 objects in Earth orbit for the United States, 3,911 of them debris.

What makes up this fleet

Recorded as "United States", the largest fleet among 99 owners. Starlink makes up 11,184 of them; the other 1,444 are not Starlink.

Satellites, rocket bodies and debris in orbit

The United States has 18,487 objects in Earth orbit in CelesTrak's whole catalogue of 31 October 2026, 08:30 UTC: 13,098 active satellites, 798 inactive satellites, 679 rocket bodies, 3,911 pieces of debris and 1 unknown object. That debris is 31.2 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit, the 2nd most of any owner. The 2-hourly orbit data of 31 October 2026, 21:10 UTC has 12,628 active (470 fewer; why they differ).
