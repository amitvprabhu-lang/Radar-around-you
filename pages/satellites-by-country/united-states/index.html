How many satellites does the United States have?

As of 14 November 2026, 11:20 UTC, the catalogue records 12,652 active satellites for the United States, 74.9 percent of the catalogue's active satellites. The whole catalogue lists 18,509 objects in Earth orbit for the United States, 3,909 of them debris.

What makes up this fleet

Recorded as "United States", the largest fleet among 99 owners. Starlink makes up 11,191 of them; the other 1,461 are not Starlink.

Satellites, rocket bodies and debris in orbit

The United States has 18,509 objects in Earth orbit in CelesTrak's whole catalogue of 13 November 2026, 07:20 UTC: 13,120 active satellites, 799 inactive satellites, 680 rocket bodies, 3,909 pieces of debris and 1 unknown object. That debris is 31.2 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit, the 2nd most of any owner. The 2-hourly orbit data of 14 November 2026, 11:20 UTC has 12,652 active (468 fewer; why they differ).
