A negative Bz means the field points south. NOAA's aurora tutorial says that as the solar wind increases in speed and the interplanetary magnetic field turns southward, geomagnetic activity increases and the aurora becomes brighter, more active, and moves further from the poles. In this data the latest Bz is not negative, and the lowest in the range is -1.4 nT.

NOAA's aurora forecast grid

NOAA's aurora forecast grid (the OVATION model, which NOAA describes as a 30 to 90 minute forecast) for 11 October 2026, 04:33 UTC, from observations at 11 October 2026, 03:49 UTC. It holds a value from 0 to 100 for each degree of latitude and longitude. NOAA says an estimate of aurora viewing probability can be derived by assuming a linear relationship to this value; we show the values as NOAA gives them and do not call them a probability. The threshold of 10 is our choice.

What it means for where you are

This page does not turn Kp into a latitude. NOAA's scales page lists, for each G level, where aurora has been seen, and gives typical latitudes in geomagnetic latitude, which is measured from the magnetic pole and is not the same as a place's ordinary latitude. NOAA's aurora tutorial also says that when the Kp index is high, between 7 and 9, the aurora will be bright and the auroral oval will move to lower latitudes. Your cloud cover and how dark your sky is also decide what you can see.

How this page is made

  • Kp: NOAA's planetary K index file, read by our collector every 30 minutes. We say "tagged" because the time is the label NOAA gives the period.
  • Solar wind: NOAA's real-time solar wind and magnetic field files (Bz in the GSM system, the field NOAA's tutorial is normally read in; which system the tutorial means is not confirmed), read every 10 minutes.
  • The aurora grid: NOAA's OVATION forecast file, read every 15 minutes.
  • The page is published only when the Kp data is less than 8 hours old. The solar wind is shown only when it is less than 6 hours old, and the grid only when it is less than 6 hours old.

Questions

What is the Kp index right now?

The latest value in NOAA's data is Kp 4, for the three-hour period tagged 00:00 UTC on 11 October 2026.

Did Kp reach 5 in the last 48 hours?

Yes, in 3 of the 16 periods with a value; the highest was Kp 7, tagged 10 Oct, 15:00 UTC. NOAA's G1 level starts at Kp 5.

Which way is the solar wind's magnetic field pointing?

Its latest Bz value is 10.9 nT, at 11 Oct, 03:55 UTC: positive, so pointing north.

Does this page forecast aurora for my town?

No. It shows NOAA's own numbers and NOAA's own short forecast grid, with their times, and makes no forecast of its own.
