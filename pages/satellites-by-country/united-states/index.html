How many satellites does the United States have?

As of 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC, the catalogue records 12,517 active satellites for the United States, 75.0 percent of the catalogue's active satellites. The whole catalogue lists 18,350 objects in Earth orbit for the United States, 3,895 of them debris.

What makes up this fleet

Recorded as "United States", the largest fleet among 99 owners. Starlink makes up 11,129 of them; the other 1,388 are not Starlink.

Satellites, rocket bodies and debris in orbit

The United States has 18,350 objects in Earth orbit in CelesTrak's whole catalogue of 10 October 2026, 08:25 UTC: 12,970 active satellites, 802 inactive satellites, 682 rocket bodies, 3,895 pieces of debris and 1 unknown object. That debris is 31.1 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit, the 2nd most of any owner. The 2-hourly orbit data of 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC has 12,517 active (453 fewer; why they differ).
