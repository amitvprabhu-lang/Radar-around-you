Numbers last changed 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC (orbit data of 11 Oct, 02:30 UTC, catalogue of 10 Oct, 08:25 UTC). CelesTrak's catalogue records 64 objects in Earth orbit for Spain: 53 active satellites, 11 inactive satellites, 0 rocket bodies and 0 pieces of debris.
