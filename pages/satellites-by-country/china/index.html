Satellites, rocket bodies and debris in orbit

China has 6,173 objects in Earth orbit in CelesTrak's whole catalogue of 17 October 2026, 08:56 UTC: 1,562 active satellites, 73 inactive satellites, 281 rocket bodies, 4,231 pieces of debris and 26 unknown objects. That debris is 33.7 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit, the most of any owner. The 2-hourly orbit data of 17 October 2026, 22:30 UTC has 1,558 active (4 fewer; why they differ).
