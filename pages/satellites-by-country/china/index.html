As of 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC, the catalogue records 1,539 active satellites for China, 9.2 percent of the catalogue's active satellites. The whole catalogue lists 6,149 objects in Earth orbit for China, 4,225 of them debris.

Satellites, rocket bodies and debris in orbit

China has 6,149 objects in Earth orbit in CelesTrak's whole catalogue of 10 October 2026, 08:25 UTC: 1,543 active satellites, 74 inactive satellites, 281 rocket bodies, 4,225 pieces of debris and 26 unknown objects. That debris is 33.7 percent of all catalogued debris in Earth orbit, the most of any owner. The 2-hourly orbit data of 11 October 2026, 02:30 UTC has 1,539 active (4 fewer; why they differ).
